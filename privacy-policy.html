RouteGuard Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

RouteGuard is a community-driven road safety application developed by VLabs. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your data.

Information We Collect

  • Name (if provided)
  • Email address
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Device location (only when required)
  • Reports submitted by users
  • Photos uploaded with reports (optional)

How We Use Your Information

  • Display nearby road alerts.
  • Improve road safety.
  • Verify community reports.
  • Prevent spam and abuse.
  • Provide a better user experience.

Location Permission

RouteGuard requests location permission only to allow users to create accurate road reports and to display nearby road events. Location is never collected continuously in the background.

Camera Permission

Camera permission is used only if the user chooses to upload a photo while creating a report.

Data Sharing

RouteGuard does not sell or rent personal information. Community reports may be visible to other users to improve road awareness.

Data Security

We use Firebase services to securely store application data. Reasonable measures are taken to protect user information.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • OpenStreetMap

Your Rights

You may update or remove your account information at any time through the application.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
